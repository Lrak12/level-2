# Island game challenge progression

Level 1 is being built by the team and is not part of this project setup.

## Implemented

| Level | Objective | Limit | Respawn points | Weather |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2: Defend the Island | Defeat 4 enemies | 180 seconds, 3 lives | Start, village approach, northern path | Recurring tropical rain |

Each enemy awards 100 points. Reaching a new checkpoint awards 25 points. Completing a level awards two points for each remaining second. The player's limited stamina makes continuous sprinting and repeated dashes impossible. Press Q for a short dash, which avoids damage during the dash.

## Planned later levels

| Level | Proposed challenge | New pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3: Mountain Pass | Defend two routes while defeating waves of enemies | Enemies approach from separate paths; checkpoints sit after each wave. |
| 4: Storm Coast | Reach the extraction point and defeat a final guard | Frequent rain reduces visibility and the extraction route must be completed before the timer ends. |

Keep three grounded checkpoints in each added level and raise difficulty by changing objectives and enemy behavior rather than removing the player's basic movement and dash controls.
